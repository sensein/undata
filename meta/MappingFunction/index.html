    Class: MappingFunction

    A directed transformation between a source DataElement and a target DataElement. May be identity (exact synonym) or a typed transformation (unit conversion, scaling, structural). System-inferred mappings are created with status=pending_curation and attributed_to=urn:undata:system.

    URI: sssom:Mapping
