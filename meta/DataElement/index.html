    Class: DataElement

    A named, typed field in a schema. The atomic unit of the undata knowledge graph. Every DataElement has a URI, a value type, and an optional ontological anchor (semantic_graph). Object-typed elements carry a schema_ref FK pointing to the DynamicSchema that defines their structure.
